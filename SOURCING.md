# Sourcing backlog: real quotes

Candidate quotes for the real-clip catalog. **None of these are in the app.** A quote gets into
`src/catalog/real-clips.json` only after someone finds the original recording, cuts one contiguous
window with the ingest tool, transcribes it word for word, and signs the review. `npm run validate`
enforces the rest.

These came from two lists generated by another AI assistant. Those lists mix verbatim quotes,
paraphrases, and lines I can't match to any real statement. Their "Context:" notes are also mostly
commentary ("widely criticized as…"), not facts about the recording. **Don't copy any of it into
`topic` or `quoteExact`.** Write those from what you hear in the source.

The notes below are my best recollection as of this writing. They are leads to check, not facts.

## Status key

- **Clip candidate:** a recording very likely exists. Find it, then cut and transcribe.
- **Text only:** the original is a tweet, IM, filing, letter or blog post. There's no audio, so under
  it can't be an audio clip, so it becomes a receipt instead (see the bottom).
- **Verify:** I couldn't place the exact wording or source. Find a primary source before doing anything else.
- **Likely not real:** I can't match it to anything, and the stated source doesn't fit. Don't use it as written.
